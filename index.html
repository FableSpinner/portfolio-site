The Dossier

Robert Vickery II is a writer, higher education professor, and recording artist rooted on the Gulf Coast. His creative prose navigates Southern Gothic realism, regional folklore recovery, and the psychological weight of isolation.

Under the musical banner of The Fable Spinner, his audio productions weave together narrative verse, Southern rhythm, and minimalist instrumentation.

Whether examining archival period literature, coordinating student success initiatives, or drafting high-tension fiction, his work tallies the friction between endurance, place, and hard-earned grace.
