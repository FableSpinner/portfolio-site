An Examination of Fallow Lines, Industrial Rust, and Southern Dark Lore.

Robert Vickery is an American author, poet, lyricist, and educator based on the Gulf Coast. His narrative prose navigates the friction of modern working-class resilience, industrial horror, and Southern Gothic folklore—specializing in subversive fairy tale deconstructions that replace domestic comfort with mechanical peril and hard-baked realism.

His short fiction has been recognized in competitive international showcases including CommuterLit (ranking in the top five of fifty-five judged selections) and anthologized through Dark Helix Press. In poetry and epistolary litanies, his work interrogates unreciprocated labor, human fragility, and theological silence.

In addition to his literary writing, he creates independent hip-hop and spoken-word poetry under the moniker White Boy Vick, distributed internationally via UnitedMasters. He serves on the instructional humanities faculty in English and Philosophy at Bishop State, directing student initiatives focused on narrative literacy, civic engagement, and community equity.
