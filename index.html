The Dossier

Robert Vickery is an American author, poet, lyricist, songwriter, and educator rooted on the Gulf Coast. Housing his multidisciplinary work under the Vickerian imprint, his creative prose navigates Southern Gothic realism, regional folklore recovery, and the psychological weight of isolation.

Beyond the page, his work bridges higher education leadership, archival scholarship, campus food security initiatives, and community coalition building across Alabama and the Gulf Coast.

Under the songwriting project Vickery & The Fable Spinners, his lyricism merges narrative verse, acoustic folk tradition, and minimal percussion.
