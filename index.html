The Dossier

Robert Vickery II is an American author, essayist, poet, lyricist, and tenured professor originally from Montgomery, Alabama. His fiction and scholarship traverse Southern Gothic realism, psychological suspense, and the darker, subversive side of classic fairy tales. Bringing sixteen years of higher education instruction in English, literature, and philosophy, his research centers on Gulf Coast Somatic Conjure Theory, Southern material culture, and Mobile Hoodoo traditions, building upon Zora Neale Hurston's ethnography. He lives along the Gulf Coast with his rescue dog, Zeus. Author portfolio and portfolio works on FanStory: Robert Vickery II on FanStory. Creative archive: vickerian.com.
