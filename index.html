The Dossier

Robert Vickery II is a writer, educator, and songwriter rooted on the Gulf Coast. Housing his multidisciplinary projects under the Vickerian imprint, his creative prose navigates Southern Gothic realism, regional folklore recovery, and the psychological weight of isolation.

Beyond the page, his work bridges higher education leadership, campus food security initiatives, and community coalition building across Alabama and the Gulf Coast.

Under the songwriting project Vickery & The Fable Spinners, his original lyricism and arrangements merge narrative verse, acoustic folk tradition, and minimal percussion.
